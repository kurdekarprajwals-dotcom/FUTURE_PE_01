# Green Leaf Cafe — Products and Services

## Project Information

* **Business Name:** Green Leaf Cafe
* **Business Type:** Fictional local cafe
* **Location:** Shivamogga, Karnataka
* **Target Audience:** College students, working professionals, friends, and families.

## 1. Coffee and Tea

### What We Offer

Explore a selection of coffee and tea for your everyday breaks. These drinks can be enjoyed while meeting friends, relaxing, or taking a break from college or work.

### Why Choose This Service?

Coffee and tea provide a simple option for customers looking to enjoy a drink during their cafe visit.

## 2. Snacks and Sandwiches

### What We Offer

Explore snack and sandwich options for a quick bite or a casual meal. Customers can choose from the available menu according to their preferences.

### Why Choose This Service?

These options are suitable for customers looking for something to eat during a short break, a meeting with friends, or a casual visit.

## 3. Desserts

### What We Offer

Discover dessert options to enjoy alongside your favourite drink or as a sweet treat during your visit.

### Why Choose This Service?

Desserts give customers another option to explore when they want something sweet with their drinks or snacks.

## 4. Cafe Experience

### What We Offer

Green Leaf Cafe is designed as a welcoming place for casual conversations, meeting friends, and taking a break from daily activities.

### Why Choose This Service?

The cafe concept caters to different occasions, including student breaks, informal meetups, and time spent with family.

## 5. Why Choose Green Leaf Cafe?

* **Different menu categories:** Explore drinks, snacks, sandwiches, and desserts.
* **Suitable for everyday breaks:** A cafe concept for customers taking a break from college or work.
* **A place to connect:** Designed for casual conversations and meeting friends.
* **Options for different occasions:** Suitable for individual visits, informal meetups, and family outings.
* **Simple website navigation:** Clear service categories help visitors understand the cafe's offerings.
