Prompt 1
Act as a professional website copywriter.

Create website content for a fictional local business named Green Leaf Cafe in Shivamogga, Karnataka.

The target customers are college students, working professionals, and families.

The cafe offers coffee, tea, snacks, sandwiches, and desserts.

Create the following website content:

Homepage headline and introduction.
About Us section.
Products and services descriptions.
Why Choose Us section.
Contact section.
Call-to-action buttons to encourage customers to visit.

Use simple, friendly, professional English. Make the content suitable for a real business website. Avoid unsupported claims.

After generating the content, explain how the prompt helped produce the result.

Prompt 2
Act as a professional website copywriter specializing in local businesses.

Improve the website content for Green Leaf Cafe in Shivamogga, Karnataka.

Target customers:

College students
Working professionals
Families

Products:

Coffee and tea
Snacks and sandwiches
Desserts

Create the following sections:

Three alternative homepage headlines.
An attractive homepage introduction.
An About Us section.
Product and service descriptions organized into categories.
A Why Choose Us section.
Five call-to-action button labels.
A Contact section with placeholders for the phone number, address, and opening hours.

Requirements:

Use simple, natural English.
Keep paragraphs short.
Use a friendly and professional tone.
Avoid unsupported claims.
Do not invent contact details.

At the end, explain five improvements that make this content effective for a business website.
