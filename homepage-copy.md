## 3. About Us

### A Cafe for Every Little Moment

Green Leaf Cafe is a fictional local cafe concept created for this website copywriting project.

Our goal is to present a welcoming place where people can enjoy their favourite drinks, share conversations, and take a break from their busy schedules.

From a quick cup of tea to an afternoon snack with friends, Green Leaf Cafe is designed around simple food, friendly communication, and everyday moments.

## 8. Content Writing Notes

The content uses simple, friendly English and short paragraphs.

The headline is memorable and communicates the cafe experience.

The products are organized into clear categories to help visitors understand the available offerings.

Contact information uses placeholders instead of invented details.

The content is structured for a business website and targets college students, working professionals, and families.
